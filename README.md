# Welcome to the ReciELO Repository!

### Introduction and Why ReciELO?
Imagine a busy city, where people are juggling their everyday lives, trying to stay organized. In the middle of all this, a group of passionate individuals is working on something different. They are brainstorming ideas, sharing experiences, and collaborating on a solution that will bring structure and support to their community. As they discuss, they are realizing that the key to solving these challenges lies in simplifying the process. We are creating Recielo, a platform that is helping people stay connected and organized.
